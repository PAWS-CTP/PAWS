🐾 PAWS

A social platform for pets and pet lovers

📌 Overview

PAWS is a community-driven platform inspired by Instagram, but designed specifically for pets and their owners. Users can share photos, videos, and stories of their pets, connect with other pet enthusiasts, and even organize meetups for pet playdates and events.

Our mission is to create a fun, engaging, and safe space where pets are the stars and their owners can bond over shared experiences.

✨ Features

Pet Profiles – Create profiles for your pets and showcase their personalities.

Posts & Sharing – Upload photos, videos, and stories of your pets.

Likes & Comments – Interact with other pet owners through likes, comments, and shares.

Events & Playdates – Organize or join local events to meet fellow pet lovers and set up pet playdates.

Community Feed – Discover trending pets, posts, and upcoming events.

🛠️ Tech Stack

Frontend: React + TailwindCSS + Next.JS

Backend: Node.js / Express

Database: Supabase

Authentication: Supabase

Hosting: Vercel
